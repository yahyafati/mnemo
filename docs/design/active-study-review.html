Concept & Definition
Prompt

What is the role of Long-Term Potentiation (LTP) in the hippocampus, and how does synaptic plasticity enable memory consolidation?

Target Resolution FSRS v4

LTP is the persistent strengthening of synapses based on recent patterns of activity. High-frequency stimulation induces NMDA receptor activation, allowing Ca²⁺ influx and subsequent insertion of AMPA receptors into the postsynaptic membrane.

lightbulb
Key Consolidation Factor

Early-phase LTP lasts hours; late-phase requires gene expression and protein synthesis, permanently restructuring hippocampal-cortical memory circuits.
