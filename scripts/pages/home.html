A few minutes a day

Look at a card, try to remember, then say how it went. Mnemo works out when to ask you again: soon for the hard ones, much later for the ones you know.

Cards in seconds

Write your own cards, bring the decks you made in Anki, or connect an AI service and let it suggest cards from your notes, a PDF or a web page. You choose which ones to keep.

See yourself improve

A calendar of the days you studied, how much you remember, and what is coming up tomorrow.
