# The Water Cycle

Water moves between the oceans, the air and the land in a continuous loop. The sun warms the surface of the sea and the water evaporates into the air as vapour.

High in the sky the vapour cools and condenses into tiny droplets, which gather as clouds. When the droplets grow heavy they fall as rain, snow or hail, and the water collects again in rivers, lakes and the ground.
