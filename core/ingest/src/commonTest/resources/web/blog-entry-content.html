Why I stopped using flashcards for vocabulary

For three years I reviewed 2,000 words a month. Then I noticed something odd: I could pass every review and still not understand a paragraph.

Three things changed my mind:

  1. Words alone have no context.
  2. Reviews reward recognition, not use.
  3. The cards I wrote myself stuck; the downloaded ones did not.

What I do now

I read, I mark a word only when it blocks me, and I write the sentence it appeared in on the card.
