City council approves new tram line

By A. Reporter · 2 October 2026

The city council voted 9 to 4 on Thursday to build a new tram line linking the harbour with the northern suburbs, ending a debate that began in 2019.

What happens next

Construction is expected to start in spring, after the tender closes. The council has set aside the following budget:

  • Tracks and stations: 120 million
  • Depot: 18 million
  • Contingency: 12 million

This is the biggest investment in public transport the city has made in fifty years.

Opponents argued that the money would be better spent on buses. Read the full timeline of the project.
